Common Questions

Airport Pickup FAQs

Do you service Hartsfield-Jackson Atlanta International Airport (ATL)?

Yes. We provide luxury black SUV pickup and drop-off service for all commercial concourses at Hartsfield-Jackson Atlanta International Airport (ATL), including domestic and international terminals. We also service private aviation FBOs and regional executive airports across the Metro Atlanta area.

How much does an airport pickup in Atlanta cost?

We use flat-rate fares based on your route — no surge pricing, no meter running. Use the Rates & Estimator page for an instant fare range, or contact us directly for a confirmed quote on your specific route.

What happens if my flight is delayed?

We monitor your flight in real time. If your flight is delayed or arrives early, your chauffeur adjusts accordingly — there is no need to call us. We include up to 45 minutes of complimentary wait time after your scheduled arrival for domestic flights, and 60 minutes for international arrivals.

Do you offer Meet & Greet service inside the terminal?

Yes. Our Meet & Greet upgrade places your chauffeur inside the terminal at baggage claim, holding a personalized name sign. They will assist with your luggage and escort you directly to the waiting vehicle. This service is available for an additional fee — mention it when booking.

How far in advance should I book an airport transfer?

We recommend booking at least 24 hours in advance to guarantee vehicle availability. For early morning flights (before 6 AM) or during peak travel periods, booking 48–72 hours ahead is strongly advised. Same-day bookings may be available — contact us directly to check.

Common Questions

Safe Driver Pickup FAQs

What is a safe driver pickup service?

A safe driver pickup is a designated driver service where a
professional chauffeur picks you up — typically after a night out,
social event, or when you simply prefer not to drive. Instead of
rideshare, you travel in a premium black SUV with a vetted, licensed
chauffeur who knows Atlanta's roads and traffic patterns.

How much does a safe driver pickup cost in Atlanta?

Safe driver pickup service starts at $180. The final price depends
on distance, time of night, and whether it is a round-trip or
multi-stop arrangement. Contact us with your specific pickup and
drop-off details for an accurate quote.

Can I use this service for long-distance trips from Atlanta?

Yes. Our safe driver service covers long-distance routes as well —
including trips from Atlanta to destinations like Savannah,
Charlotte, or Birmingham. The Lincoln Navigator's spacious, quiet
cabin makes it ideal for comfortable long-haul travel. Contact us to
book a long-distance route.

Is this service available late at night or on weekends?

Yes. We offer late-night and weekend pickups to accommodate nights
out, events, and celebrations across Atlanta. We recommend booking
in advance for Friday and Saturday evenings as these are our
highest-demand time slots.

How is this different from Uber or Lyft?

Unlike rideshare platforms, our chauffeurs are background-checked,
professionally trained, and personally vetted. You ride in a
late-model premium SUV — not a personal vehicle. There is no surge
pricing, no uncertainty about who is picking you up, and our
chauffeur has expert knowledge of Atlanta traffic routes that no
algorithm can replicate.

Common Questions

Signature Accounts FAQs

What is a Signature Account?

A Signature Account is a recurring transportation arrangement for clients who need Posh Limousines on a regular basis — weekly, bi-monthly, or monthly. Your routes, preferences, and billing details are kept on file, and your rides are prioritized in our dispatch schedule. It eliminates the friction of booking one trip at a time.

How often do I need to ride to qualify?

There is no strict minimum. Signature Accounts are open to any client who rides on a predictable recurring schedule, whether that's twice a week or once a month. If your transportation needs are consistent and you value reliability, a Signature Account is for you. Contact us and we'll tailor an arrangement to your schedule.

Can I have the same chauffeur for every ride?

We do our best to assign a consistent preferred chauffeur to Signature Account clients. While scheduling and availability can occasionally require a different driver, all of our chauffeurs are vetted, trained, and briefed on your preferences — so the experience remains seamless regardless of who is behind the wheel.

How does billing work for a Signature Account?

Signature Account clients can choose between weekly or monthly invoicing, eliminating the need to pay after each individual trip. Accepted payment methods include Cash, Venmo, Zelle, and major credit cards (a small processing fee applies to card payments). Contact us to set up your preferred billing cycle.

Are Signature Account clients eligible for Posh Preferred rewards?

Yes. Signature Account clients are automatically enrolled in our Posh Preferred referral rewards program. It is our way of rewarding clients who trust us with their transportation and share that experience with others.

Common Questions

Posh Preferred FAQs

How do I join Posh Preferred?

All active Signature Account clients are automatically enrolled in Posh Preferred. If you are not yet a Signature Account client, opening one is the first step. You can also contact us directly — if your booking history qualifies you, we will invite you to join. Membership is personal, not automatic for every client.

What counts as a referral?

A referral is any new client who books and completes a trip with Posh Limousines and mentions your name at the time of booking. The trip must be completed — not just booked — for it to count toward your reward tally. Round trips count as 2 trips, so 5 round trips reach the 10-trip threshold.

When is my 25% discount applied?

Once your 10th referred trip — or 5th round trip — is completed, we will contact you to confirm a 25% discount has been credited to your account. It is applied automatically to your next booking. No code or voucher is needed. After it is used, your referral count resets and you can begin earning the next reward.

Is there a limit to how many discounts I can earn?

No. There is no cap on the number of Posh Preferred discounts you can earn. For every additional 10 single trips (or 5 round trips) you refer, you earn another 25% discount on a future booking. The more you share Posh, the more you save.

Can the discount be applied to any service?

Yes. The 25% Posh Preferred discount can be applied to any single booking — airport pickup, corporate event, formal celebration, casual outing, or safe driver pickup. It cannot be combined with other active promotions or discounts, but it applies to any service and any booking size.
