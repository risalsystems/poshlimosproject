Posh Preferred

Earn Rewards for Sharing Posh

Posh Preferred is our exclusive referral rewards program for a select group of loyal clients. When you love a service, you naturally share it — and we believe you should be rewarded every time you do. For every 10 trips (or 5 round trips) you refer to Posh Limousines of Atlanta, you receive 25% off your next booking.

This is not a points system. There are no expiration dates. There is no app to download. Posh Preferred works the way luxury should — simply, personally, and without friction.

How Posh Preferred Works

  • Refer a friend, colleague, or client to Posh Limousines
  • Ask them to mention your name when they book
  • Each completed trip they take counts toward your reward
  • Every 10 single trips or 5 round trips = 25% off your next booking
  • No cap — keep referring, keep earning
  • We track your referrals — no paperwork on your end

Who Qualifies for Posh Preferred

Posh Preferred is extended to our most loyal and trusted clients — those who have chosen Posh Limousines of Atlanta as their preferred transportation partner. All active Signature Account holders are automatically enrolled. New clients who demonstrate consistent use may be personally invited to join at our discretion.

If you believe you qualify or would like to be considered, reach out to us directly. Membership is personal, not automated — which is what makes it worth having.

Counting Your Referrals

When you refer someone to Posh Limousines of Atlanta, simply ask them to mention your name when they book. We log every completed trip tied to your referral and notify you the moment your discount threshold is reached. Your 25% discount is then applied automatically to your next booking — no code or voucher needed.

Round trips count as 2 trips toward the 10-trip threshold, so referring 5 round trips is all it takes to earn your reward.
