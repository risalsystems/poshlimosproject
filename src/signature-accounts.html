Recurring Client Services

Signature Accounts

Your Dedicated Chauffeur Service

Some clients don't just need a ride — they need a reliable transportation partner. Posh Limousines of Atlanta's Signature Account program is built for clients who depend on premium chauffeur service on a recurring basis: weekly commuters, twice-a-month regulars, and monthly clients who expect the same standard of excellence every single ride.

As a Signature Account client, your preferences are on file, your routes are memorized, and your chauffeur is briefed before every ride. No repeating your destination. No re-explaining your expectations. Just seamless service, every time you need it.

Signature Account Benefits

  • Priority scheduling — your rides are reserved first
  • Preferred chauffeur consistency on repeat bookings
  • Simplified billing — invoiced weekly or monthly
  • No surge pricing — locked-in rates for your routes
  • Dedicated account contact for last-minute changes
  • Automatic eligibility for Posh Preferred referral rewards

Who Signature Accounts Are For

Our Signature Account clients include corporate executives who need airport runs, for example, every Monday and Thursday, healthcare professionals with demanding early-morning schedules, business owners who attend recurring client dinners, and golf enthusiasts with annual Masters badges who count on seamless Atlanta-to-Augusta transfers each April. If you have outgrown the inconsistency of on-demand rideshare, a Signature Account is built for you.

Whether your cadence is twice a week or once a month, a Signature Account ensures your transportation is handled — quietly, reliably, and to the standard you expect.

Posh Preferred: Rewards for Your Referrals

As a Signature Account client, you're automatically enrolled in our exclusive Posh Preferred program. Refer friends, colleagues, or clients — and earn a 25% discount on your next booking! Common Questions

Signature Accounts FAQs

A Signature Account is a recurring transportation arrangement for clients who need Posh Limousines on a regular basis — weekly, bi-monthly, or monthly. Your routes, preferences, and billing details are kept on file, and your rides are prioritized in our dispatch schedule. It eliminates the friction of booking one trip at a time.

There is no strict minimum. Signature Accounts are open to any client who rides on a predictable recurring schedule, whether that's twice a week or once a month. If your transportation needs are consistent and you value reliability, a Signature Account is for you. Contact us and we'll tailor an arrangement to your schedule.

We do our best to assign a consistent preferred chauffeur to Signature Account clients. While scheduling and availability can occasionally require a different driver, all of our chauffeurs are vetted, trained, and briefed on your preferences — so the experience remains seamless regardless of who is behind the wheel.

Signature Account clients can choose between weekly or monthly invoicing, eliminating the need to pay after each individual trip. Accepted payment methods include Cash, Venmo, Zelle, and major credit cards (a small processing fee applies to card payments). Contact us to set up your preferred billing cycle.

Yes. Signature Account clients are automatically enrolled in our Posh Preferred referral rewards program. For every 10 single trips — or 5 round-trips — you refer to Posh Limousines, you receive a 25% discount on your next booking. It is our way of rewarding clients who trust us with their transportation and share that experience with others.
